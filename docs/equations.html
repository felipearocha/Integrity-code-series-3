Scope of sourcing. This package is a self-contained classical-dynamics lap model. The equations below are standard textbook mechanics (Newton's second law, the aerodynamic force law, a first-order lag filter, and a Gaussian window) combined with engineering coefficients chosen for the model. No external standard or paper is claimed as a source; every relation and constant reproduced here is transcribed verbatim from this repository's own README.md and src/ code — nothing has been added that is not already in the repository. Relations that are standard physics are tagged [SOURCE] (classical mechanics); model-specific proxies and tuned coefficients are tagged [ASSUMED].

1. Aerodynamics — Mode Filter & Forces

1.1 Aero-mode filter (spatial ODE)

$$ \frac{dm}{ds} \;=\; \frac{m_{\text{raw}} - m}{\tau_{\text{aero}}}, \qquad \tau_{\text{aero}} = 40\ \text{m} $$

[ASSUMED] First-order spatial lag that smooths the discrete high-downforce / low-drag mode switch along arc length; smoothing length is a model parameter.

Code: src/simulation/physics_model.py → run_lap (aero-mode filter step).

1.2 Aero-coefficient blend

$$ C_d \;=\; (1-m)\,C_{d,Z} + m\,C_{d,X}, \qquad C_l \;=\; (1-m)\,C_{l,Z} + m\,C_{l,X} $$ with $m=0$ the high-downforce state and $m=1$ the low-drag state.

[ASSUMED] Linear blend of the two aero configurations; coefficient values are model parameters (CarParams).

Code: src/simulation/physics_model.py → aero_coeffs.

1.3 Aerodynamic drag and downforce

$$ F_{\text{drag}} \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\,\rho\,C_d(m)\,A\,v^{2}, \qquad F_{\text{down}} \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\,\rho\,C_l(m)\,A\,v^{2} $$

[SOURCE] Classical aerodynamic force law (quadratic in speed); air density $\rho$ constant.

Code: src/simulation/physics_model.py → run_lap (aero forces).

2. Traction Ceiling & Slip-Angle Proxy

2.1 Friction-limited traction ceiling

$$ F_{\text{trac,max}} \;=\; \mu_{\text{eff}}\,F_{\text{down}}\cdot 4 $$ the factor 4 aggregates the four tyres.

[ASSUMED] Coulomb friction limit applied to the aggregate downforce over four contact patches.

Code: src/simulation/physics_model.py → run_lap (traction ceiling).

2.2 Lateral slip-angle proxy

$$ \beta \;=\; \operatorname{clip}\!\left(0.35\,\arctan\!\frac{v^{2}\kappa}{g},\; -0.25,\; 0.25\right) $$ with $\kappa$ the local track curvature and $g$ gravity.

[ASSUMED] Curvature-driven proxy for lateral slip angle; gain and clip bounds are model parameters.

Code: src/simulation/physics_model.py → run_lap (slip angle proxy).

3. Master Equation of Motion

$$ m_{\text{car}}\,\frac{dv}{dt} \;=\; F_{\text{drive}} - F_{\text{drag}} - F_{\text{roll}} - F_{\text{grade}} - F_{\text{brake}} $$ with the resistive terms $$ F_{\text{roll}} \;=\; C_{rr}\,m_{\text{car}}\,g\,\cos\theta, \qquad F_{\text{grade}} \;=\; m_{\text{car}}\,g\,\sin\theta $$ and $F_{\text{drive}} = \min\!\bigl(F_{\text{trac,max}},\, P_{\text{drive}}/v\bigr)$, $\theta$ the local grade angle.

[SOURCE] Newton's second law for longitudinal vehicle dynamics; rolling resistance and grade are standard resistive forces.

Code: src/simulation/physics_model.py → run_lap (longitudinal forces, velocity update).

4. Tyre Thermal Model & Grip Window

4.1 Tyre bulk-temperature ODE

$$ \frac{dT}{dt} \;=\; k_{\text{heat}}\,E_{\text{slip}} - k_{\text{cool}}\,(T - T_{\text{track}}), \qquad E_{\text{slip}} \;=\; \bigl(|\beta| + 0.25\,b\bigr)\,v^{2}\cdot 0.1 $$ with $b$ the brake proxy and $T_{\text{track}}$ the track surface temperature.

[ASSUMED] Single-bulk (lumped-capacitance) tyre temperature — no radial gradient; heating/cooling coefficients and the slip-energy scaling are model parameters.

Code: src/simulation/physics_model.py → run_lap (tyre temperature ODE).

4.2 Gaussian thermal grip window

$$ f_T \;=\; \exp\!\left(-\frac{(T - T_{\text{opt}})^{2}}{2\,\sigma^{2}}\right), \qquad \mu_{\text{eff}} \;=\; \mu_0\,f_T\,(1 - k_w\,\text{wear}) $$ grip peaks at $T=T_{\text{opt}}$ ($f_T=1$) and falls off over the thermal half-width $\sigma$.

[ASSUMED] Gaussian grip-temperature window with a linear wear-degradation factor; $T_{\text{opt}}$, $\sigma$, $\mu_0$, $k_w$ are model parameters.

Code: src/simulation/physics_model.py → grip_window, mu_effective.

5. Tyre Wear

$$ \frac{dw}{dt} \;=\; k_w\,E_{\text{slip}}\,\bigl[\,0.6 + 0.8\,(1 - f_T)\,\bigr] $$ wear accumulates monotonically and accelerates away from the optimum grip temperature.

[ASSUMED] Slip-energy-driven wear with a thermal penalty bracket; coefficients are model parameters.

Code: src/simulation/physics_model.py → run_lap (wear ODE).

6. ERS State of Charge

$$ \frac{d\,\text{SOC}}{dt} \;=\; -\frac{P_{\text{bat}}}{E_{\text{bat}}} $$ with $P_{\text{bat}}>0$ on deploy and $P_{\text{bat}}<0$ on regeneration; deploy is gated on SOC and braking is the regen trigger.

[ASSUMED] Battery energy balance with a deploy/regen gate; power ceilings ($P_{\text{MGU-K,max}}$, $P_{\text{regen,max}}$) and $E_{\text{bat}}$ are model parameters.

Code: src/simulation/physics_model.py → ers_power, run_lap (SOC ODE).

7. Space-Marched Integration

$$ \Delta t_i \;=\; \frac{\Delta s}{v_i\,\cos\beta_i + \varepsilon}, \qquad t_{\text{lap}} \;=\; \sum_i \Delta t_i $$ the six state variables ($v,\ \beta,\ \text{SOC},\ \text{fuel},\ T_{\text{tire}},\ \text{wear}$) are advanced node-by-node along arc length $s$; the local time step follows from the along-track speed, and $\varepsilon$ guards against division by zero.

[SOURCE] Explicit space-marched (forward-Euler in $s$) integration; step $\Delta s = L/N$.

Code: src/simulation/physics_model.py → run_lap (integration loop).
